Important Notice & Privacy Consent

Before using Adobe Stock Metadata Pro, please review how data is processed:

  • AI Metadata Generation: Your selected stock image and metadata prompt are processed within your active Google Gemini web session under your personal Google account. We do not store, intercept, or send your images to third-party servers.
  • Licensing & Device Verification: When activating a license, a pseudonymous installation identifier and your license code are validated with our backend to enforce device limits.
  • No Automatic Submission: This extension never automatically submits or publishes your stock assets. You retain full review and submission control.
